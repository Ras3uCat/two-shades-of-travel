Section E

Conflicts & Gaps

Conflict
Luxury inspiration vs. accessible brand positioning
3 of 5 inspiration sites (jeskojets, snamitravel, calderagroup) are high-end luxury brands — dark palettes, minimal copy, price-agnostic, exclusive tone. TSoT serves overwhelmed Millennial parents who need warmth and accessibility, not gatekeeping.
Resolution: Borrow visual craft (full-bleed photography, editorial type, generous white space). Reject exclusivity signals (dark backgrounds, opaque pricing, elite-only language). The Warm brief word and Keke Palmer's inclusive energy are the tiebreakers.
Conflict
"Creative" personality implementation risk
Raspucat's "creative" personality can render with rounded bubbles, bright illustrations, or playful animations. The inspiration sites are cinematic and editorial — not playful. Ensure AntiGravity reads this as editorial creative, not fun/playful creative.
Resolution: No illustrations, no rounded cartoon-style elements. Photography as the design. Typography as the art direction. Animations are subtle fades and parallax, not bounces or pops.
