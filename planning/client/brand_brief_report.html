Section 03

Celebrity Aesthetic

"Anthony Bourdain meets Keke Palmer"

Creative Direction Reference

Anthony Bourdain

Raw authenticity. Gritty, real-world travel photography. No filters, no tourist traps. Documentary-style storytelling that makes you feel like you're actually there — street-level, honest, unpolished in the best way.

Keke Palmer

Vibrant energy. Unapologetic cultural pride. Bold color moments paired with genuine warmth. She makes people feel included, not talked down to — equal parts fun and substantive.
